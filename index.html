WELCOME TO OUR WORLD

A sanctuary at
the jungle's edge.

Chitwan Safari Resort was founded in 2001 with one idea: to create a peaceful sanctuary where comfort meets nature, while respecting the environment and supporting the local community.

Located in Sauraha, Ratnagar Municipality, beside the buffer zone of Chitwan National Park, the resort brings together modern hospitality and the region's rich Tharu heritage.

Whether you are travelling with family, as a couple, with friends or with a group, discover a stay shaped by nature and local experiences.

OUR STORY & VALUES

Travel thoughtfully.
Leave a lighter footprint.

Regenerative tourism is a long-term philosophy at Chitwan Safari Resort, guided by the vision of Ashok Kumar Yadav and supported by the next generation, including Gyanendra Raya.

The resort's stated approach focuses on respecting nature, supporting the Sauraha community and preserving local culture for future generations.
